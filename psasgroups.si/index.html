Architecture & Security

Engineered for Sovereign Zero-Trust Scale

Every module built with mathematical precision, audited compliance, and microsecond performance.

100% On-Premise Data Localization

Raw confidential records, patient MRIs, and financial balances never leave local customer servers. Only encrypted weights are shared.

Mathematical noise injection guarantees that model weights cannot be reverse-engineered to reconstruct individual training records.
